# Listend privacy policy — draft

Publication status: not published. Owner must select a public support contact and hosting URL and review this text before publication. This notice and the draft label are preparation notes, not part of the final policy.

## Your listening journal

Listend stores album logs, ratings, reactions, reviews, track notes, taste reflections, and recommendation history locally on your device. The main app and its Share extension use a shared local store so entries saved through the extension can appear in the app. Listend does not operate a journal account or its own cloud-sync service. Device backups may be managed separately by Apple according to your settings.

You can edit or delete journal entries in Listend. Removing the app may remove its local data. Listend currently has no built-in export feature.

## Apple Music and other Apple services

When you use connected music features, Listend communicates with Apple services to search or retrieve album information, artwork, recently played albums, recommendations, library matches, and previews. The Share extension may use Apple's album lookup service to resolve an album link. Apple receives the network requests needed to provide these services and processes information under its own policies.

Music access is controlled by iOS permissions. You can change Listend's Music access in system Settings. Some music features may be limited or unavailable without access or a working connection. Opening an Apple Music link uses Apple Music or your browser.

## SoundPrint and writing assistance

On supported devices and when enabled/available, Listend may process journal information with Apple's on-device Foundation Models to generate reflections, writing assistance, or reaction suggestions. Local fallback processing is available. The inspected app does not send your journal text to a developer-operated AI server or a third-party cloud AI provider. Generated reflections may be imperfect and are interpretations of your listening notes.

## Developer collection and tracking

The current app has no developer-operated analytics or advertising SDK, tracking identifier, or journal-upload backend. Listend does not sell journal data or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

If you send feedback or a support request, the information you choose to include can be received and used to investigate it. Please avoid including journal content you do not want to share. TestFlight and optional Apple diagnostics are handled through Apple's services and settings.
